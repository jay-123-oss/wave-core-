3. COMMERCIAL LIDAR RESAMPLING & THIN OBSTACLE PRESERVATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Commercial robotic LIDARs come in varying angular resolutions (0.25 deg to 1.0 deg) 
and beam counts. The `wave_core_resample_laserscan` algorithm downsamples arbitrary 
scans into the engine's 32 polar rays while preserving safety-critical features.

A critical engineering vulnerability in standard interpolation/averaging downsampling 
is that thin obstacles (such as 2 cm metal poles, guy wires, or chair legs) get diluted 
or erased by wide-beam averaging.

Wave-Core solves this with conservative minimum-pooling across 32 angular sectors:
$R_{out}[b] = \min_{i \in \text{Sector}(b)} (R_{raw}[i])$

| Low-cost RPLIDAR A1| 360 beams        | 1.000 deg        | 1.350 m           | YES (100%)     |
| YDLIDAR / Slamtec  | 540 beams        | 0.667 deg        | 1.350 m           | YES (100%)     |
| RPLIDAR S2 / S3    | 720 beams        | 0.500 deg        | 1.350 m           | YES (100%)     |
| Hokuyo UST-10LX    | 1080 beams       | 0.250 deg        | 1.350 m           | YES (100%)     |
+--------------------+------------------+------------------+-------------------+----------------+

O(1) Sector Lookup Optimization:
- Initial naive search: Iterated over 32 beams for every raw range reading (23,040 iterations
  per 720-beam scan, consuming ~45 us).
- Optimized sector mapping: Direct constant-time sector lookup (`angle_to_beam_index`)
  reduced resampling time to < 2.0 us.
