3. TEST TAXONOMY: 5 DIFFICULTY SCENARIO CLASSES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The 1,000 procedural scenarios were partitioned into five rigorous difficulty
classes designed to evaluate reactive slaloms, dynamic hazards, concave deadlocks,
narrow symmetric channels, and sensor degradation:

CLASS 1: DENSE PROCEDURAL POLE FORESTS (Runs 1 to 300)
- Environment: 50m x 50m enclosed arena with 4 boundary walls.
- Obstacle Layout: 30 to 70 randomly placed static cylindrical poles (r = 0.12m - 0.25m).
- Navigation Mission: Transit from (5.0, 5.0) to (45.0, 45.0) at desired v = 3.8 - 5.0 m/s.
- Challenge: Reactive high-speed slalom between closely spaced obstacles without
  sacrificing forward momentum or getting trapped in clusters.

CLASS 2: DYNAMIC CROSS-TRAFFIC & MOVING INTRUDERS (Runs 301 to 500)
- Environment: 40m x 30m arena with moving dynamic circular obstacles.
- Obstacle Dynamics: 3 to 6 dynamic obstacles moving at v = 1.2 m/s perpendicular
  to the vehicle's transit vector, bouncing within safe lateral bounds.
- Navigation Mission: Navigate from (5.0, 15.0) to (35.0, 15.0) at v = 4.0 m/s.
- Challenge: Multi-body collision avoidance, timing gap estimation, and velocity-
  aligned emergency retro-braking if an intruder intercepts the clearance window.

CLASS 3: DEEP U-TRAPS & CUL-DE-SACS (Runs 501 to 700)
- Environment: Procedurally generated concave U-shaped wall traps.
- Dimensions: Variable depths (4.0m to 6.0m) and widths (2.5m to 3.8m).
- Goal Placement: Placed directly behind the back wall of the concave dead end.
- Challenge: Local minima escape. Vehicle enters deep into trap, detects 3-quadrant
  saturation (Front, Left, Right blocked), engages ReverseEscape and 180-degree
  rotation, and exits through the entrance without freezing or oscillating.

CLASS 4: NARROW SLALOM & WINDING CORRIDORS (Runs 701 to 850)
- Environment: Straight and offset narrow corridors (lengths = 32m, widths = 1.3m - 1.8m).
- Initial Perturbation: Vehicle spawned with +- 0.10m lateral offset from centerline.
- Navigation Mission: Traverse corridor at v = 3.2 m/s.
- Challenge: Differential balance between Left and Right HDC quadrants. Vehicle must
  smoothly center along the corridor axis without ping-pong wall oscillations.

CLASS 5: SENSOR NOISE, DROPOUTS & CHAOS ENVIRONMENTS (Runs 851 to 1,000)
- Environment: 45m x 45m obstacle arena subjected to heavy sensor degradation.
- Fault Injections: Beam range jitter, 15% random dropouts, and velocity fluctuations.
- Navigation Mission: Transit from (4.0, 4.0) to (40.0, 40.0) at v = 4.2 m/s.
- Challenge: Temporal hysteresis coherence filter must suppress phantom noise spikes
  while immediately responding to real physical obstacles.

5. SENSOR FAULT INJECTION & ZERO-PANIC CHAOS RESULTS

   - Result: 0 panics, 0 crashes. Every quadrant metric (min, max, avg, median)

     sensor_frozen = true, and engaged EmergencyBrake on Frame 5. Automatically
     recovered when dynamic readings resumed.

4. 50% Sensor Blackout (Quadrant Loss):
   - Evaluated total disconnect of Right and Back quadrants (16 dead beams).
   - Result: Reflex engine maintained orientation and steered towards the confirmed
     operational left sector without confusion.

5. Linux Timing Jitter (5 ms to 25 ms):
   - Evaluated variable scheduling intervals with speed integration noise.
   - Result: 500 jitter frames in open space produced 0 false emergency brakes,
     while sudden obstacle emergence triggered EmergencyBrake in <= 1 frame.
