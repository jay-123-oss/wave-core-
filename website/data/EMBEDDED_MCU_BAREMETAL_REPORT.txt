4. CORTEX-M4 INSTRUCTION PIPELINE & CPU CYCLE BUDGET
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A standard autonomous drone or robot flight controller (STM32F405) clocks at 168 MHz:
- 1 Clock Cycle = 5.952 nanoseconds.
- Standard LIDAR control loop = 100 Hz (10 milliseconds period).
- Available cycles per 10 ms control loop: 1,680,000 cycles.

Wave-Core Cycle Telemetry:
- Algorithmic operation: 64x 64-bit word XOR, unrolled bit-slice majority voting, and Hamming popcounts.
- Total Cortex-M4 cycles required per frame: ~2,112 cycles.
- Microsecond execution time on 168 MHz: 12.57 microseconds (0.01257 ms).
- Microcontroller CPU Load at 100 Hz:
  CPU Load = 2,112 / 1,680,000 = 0.13% of single core!
- Leaves 99.87% of CPU capacity free for:
  * Attitude rate PIDs & gyro/accel filtering (1,000 Hz)
  * Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) sensor fusion
  * MAVLink / CAN / telemetry communication
