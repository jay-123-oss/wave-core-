4. DETAILED FEATURE ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. Body-to-Earth Tilt Compensation (Option 1 + Option 3):
   When a quadcopter accelerates forward, it must tilt nose-down (e.g. 20-30 degrees).
   Conventional obstacle avoidance algorithms suffer from severe false alarms because 
   the downward-tilted depth sensor mistakes the horizontal horizon for the ground.
   Wave-Core's `fuse_pointcloud` rotates every 3D depth point by the current attitude 
   quaternion $p_{\text{aligned}} = q_{\text{tilt}} \cdot p_{\text{body}}$, ensuring 
   horizontal beams remain gravity-aligned regardless of bank angle.

B. Aerodynamic Disturbance Observer (Option 3):
   High-rate IMU accelerometer residuals ($a_{\text{earth}} - g$) are filtered against 
   the rigid-body aerodynamic drag model. Any unexplained persistent lateral force is 
   isolated as wind gust disturbance ($F_{\text{wind}}$). Near the ground ($< 0.8$m), 
   rotor downwash ground effect is dynamically magnified up to 1.35x.

C. Direct Autopilot Wire Protocol (Option 2):
   No intermediate companion computer translation layers (such as MAVROS or Python 
   wrappers) are required. The library directly serializes binary MAVLink v2 frames 
   with X.25 CRC-M checksums into byte arrays, ready for immediate `write(serial_fd)` 
   to the flight controller's TELEM port at up to 100 Hz.
