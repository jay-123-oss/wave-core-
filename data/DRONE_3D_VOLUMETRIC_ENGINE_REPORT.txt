3. ARBITRARY 3D POINTCLOUD RESAMPLING & THIN HAZARD PRESERVATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Commercial 3D depth sensors (e.g. Intel RealSense D435i, Ouster OS1, Velodyne Puck) 
produce dense, irregular 3D pointclouds (`x, y, z` coordinates).

The `wave_core_resample_pointcloud_3d` function converts arbitrary pointclouds into 
the 48 spherical rays using conservative minimum-pooling in constant time:
$R_{out}[\text{ray}] = \min_{(x, y, z) \in \text{Sector}(\text{ray})} \sqrt{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}$
